Support

Chess for Apple Vision Pro, in crisp 1-bit black and white, where every capture is a stop-motion duel.

Getting started

  • Put the board on a table. Grab the bar under the board and set it down on a real table; it snaps to flat surfaces.
  • Pick up a piece. The piece your eyes or hand are on glows yellow. Pinch it to lift it into your hand. Its moves are marked with dots, or brackets for captures.
  • Put it down. Carry it over a dot and open your fingers. Drop it on an enemy piece and they fight it out. Let go anywhere else and it goes back home.
  • The panel above the far edge of the board has New Game, the CPU's strength, the Look (Iron & Stone or Robots), Colors (red vs blue, with the Robots), Undo, Sound and SharePlay.

Questions

I can't see the board.
Press and hold the Digital Crown to bring your windows back in front of you.
What does the Full Game include?
The Robots, a transforming army you can play in red vs blue or black and white; the Wizard, the strongest CPU; and hosting SharePlay matches. It's a one-time purchase, shared with Family Sharing.
I bought the Full Game but it's locked.
Tap anything with a padlock, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.
How do I play a friend?
Start a FaceTime call with them, then tap SharePlay on the panel (without a call, it offers to start one). Your friend joins from the SharePlay prompt. The person who starts the match hosts and needs the Full Game; friends join free.
Can I turn off the red and blue?
Yes. With the Robots on show, tap Colors on the panel.
